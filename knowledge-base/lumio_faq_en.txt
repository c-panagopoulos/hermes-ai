Q: What is Lumio?
A: Lumio is an online store specializing in home and lifestyle products, including furniture, décor, kitchenware, lighting, and accessories. We ship across the United States and offer easy returns.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number on our website under "Track My Order" or directly on the carrier's website.

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days. Express delivery takes 2-3 business days. Same-day delivery is available in select cities. Delivery times may vary during peak seasons.

Q: How much does shipping cost?
A: Standard shipping is $4.99. Express shipping is $12.99. Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Currently, Lumio only ships within the United States, including Alaska and Hawaii. We are working on expanding internationally in 2025.

Q: What is Lumio's return policy?
A: You can return any unused, unopened item within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging. We do not accept returns on personalized items, gift cards, or final sale products.

Q: How do I return an item?
A: Visit our website and go to "Returns & Exchanges" under your account. Select the item you wish to return, print the prepaid return label, and drop the package off at any UPS location. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the return.

Q: Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
A: Yes, exchanges are available for the same item in a different size or color, subject to availability. Start an exchange through your account under "Returns & Exchanges."

Q: When will I receive my refund?
A: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your returned item. The refund will be issued to your original payment method.

Q: My order arrived damaged. What should I do?
A: We are sorry to hear that. Please contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos of the damage. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund at no extra cost.

Q: I received the wrong item. What should I do?
A: Please contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item received. We will arrange a free return and send the correct item as soon as possible.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placing them. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be cancelled. If you miss the window, you can return the item once it arrives.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
A: Address changes are only possible within 1 hour of placing the order. Contact our support team immediately at support@lumio.com or via live chat.

Q: What payment methods does Lumio accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Lumio gift cards.

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes. All transactions on Lumio are encrypted using SSL technology and we are fully PCI-DSS compliant. We never store your full card details.

Q: Do you offer gift cards?
A: Yes, Lumio gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $200. They can be purchased online and sent via email. Gift cards do not expire.

Q: Do you have a loyalty program?
A: Yes, Lumio Rewards is our free loyalty program. You earn 1 point for every $1 spent. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future purchases. Sign up for free on our website.

Q: How do I create a Lumio account?
A: Click "Sign Up" on our website, enter your email address and create a password. You can also sign up using your Google or Apple account.

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email address. You will receive a password reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if you do not see it.

Q: How do I contact Lumio customer support?
A: You can reach us via live chat on our website (available 9am-9pm EST, Monday to Friday), by email at support@lumio.com, or by phone at 1-800-LUMIO-01. We aim to respond to all emails within 24 hours.

Q: Does Lumio offer assembly services?
A: Yes, assembly is available for select furniture items at an additional cost. You can add this service during checkout. Assembly is carried out by a certified partner and is available in most US cities.

Q: Can I buy a product that is out of stock?
A: You can sign up for restock notifications on the product page. We will email you as soon as the item is back in stock. We do not accept pre-orders at this time.

Q: Does Lumio offer discounts for bulk orders?
A: Yes, we offer discounts for orders of 10 or more units of the same product. Contact our business team at business@lumio.com for a custom quote.

Q: How do I apply a discount code?
A: Enter your discount code in the "Promo Code" field at checkout and click "Apply." Only one discount code can be used per order. Codes cannot be combined with other offers.

Q: What happens if my discount code does not work?
A: Check that the code has not expired and that your order meets any minimum requirements. If the issue persists, contact our support team and we will assist you.

Q: Does Lumio have a physical store?
A: Lumio is currently online only. We do not have physical store locations, but we are exploring pop-up experiences in select cities in 2025.

Q: Do Lumio gift cards expire?
A: No. Lumio gift cards never expire and have no fees. You can use them at any time without losing any value.

Q: Is there an expiration date on gift cards?
A: No, there is absolutely no expiration date on Lumio gift cards. They never expire.
